Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

If you have a question not listed below, please reach out to us at mimtransportation@yahoo.com.

What if I cannot afford transportation?

Memories In Motion Transportation Ministry believes that every memory deserves a visit. When financial hardship would prevent someone from visiting a loved one, transportation sponsorship assistance may be available through donations and community support.

No one will be denied service due to financial hardship. Through ministry donations and community sponsorships, assisted rates ($35–$50) and fully sponsored "Memory Visits" ($0 / free) are available so that everyone has the opportunity to honor a loved one.

Apply for Transportation Assistance

How much does a cemetery visit cost?

Our standard round-trip visit is $65–$85 per individual, which includes door-to-door transportation to and from the cemetery, up to 1 hour on-site, assistance entering/exiting the vehicle, and compassionate companionship.

For seniors, widows/widowers, veterans' families, and fixed-income individuals, we offer a Ministry Assisted Rate of $35–$50. Family group visits (2–4 members) are $95–$125, and visits to Ohio Western Reserve National Cemetery have a suggested donation of $75.

Sponsored "Memory Visits" ($0 / No charge) are available for individuals facing significant financial hardship.

View complete pricing details

Who does Memories In Motion Transportation Ministry serve?

We serve seniors, widows and widowers, veterans, individuals with disabilities, and families who wish to visit the cemetery or memorial resting place of a loved one across Solon and Northeast Ohio.

Do you provide rides to Ohio Western Reserve National Cemetery?

Yes. We proudly provide round-trip, door-to-door transportation to local, regional, and national veteran cemeteries, including the Ohio Western Reserve National Cemetery in Seville, Ohio.

Learn more about Veteran Cemetery Visits

Will someone stay with me during the cemetery visit?

Yes. Many people need not only a ride to the cemetery to visit their loved one, but someone who understands why the visit matters. Your driver waits on-site and can walk beside you to the headstone, assist with placing flowers or flags, offer a steady arm, or give you quiet privacy while you reflect and pray — never rushed.

Can we stop for flowers, wreaths, or flags along the way?

Absolutely. Simply let us know when scheduling your visit if you would like to make a brief stop at a florist or store to pick up fresh flowers, seasonal wreaths, or small American flags.

How far in advance should I schedule a visit?

We recommend requesting your ride at least 48 hours in advance so we can coordinate your pickup time, destination, and any mobility or floral stops. For major remembrance days such as Memorial Day, Veterans Day, Mother's Day, and Father's Day, we encourage booking as early as possible.

How can a church, funeral home, or donor sponsor visits?

Funeral homes, churches, civic organizations, veterans' groups, businesses, and compassionate individuals can sponsor individual visits (`$25`, `$50`, `$100`, or `$250`) or provide ongoing support to the Memories In Motion Transportation Sponsorship Fund.
