About Our Ministry

More Than a Ride — A Ministry of Remembrance

Memories In Motion Transportation Ministry is a faith-centered community outreach service dedicated to helping individuals and families visit the resting places of loved ones when transportation, mobility, or financial challenges make those visits difficult.

Founded from personal experience following the loss of a beloved husband and veteran, the ministry recognizes the comfort, healing, and connection that come from honoring loved ones through meaningful visits to their final resting place.

We understand that many individuals, particularly seniors, widows, widowers, veterans' families, and those with mobility limitations, face obstacles that make these visits difficult or impossible without assistance. Many people need not only a ride, but someone who understands why the visit matters.

“Many people need not only a ride to the cemetery, but someone who understands why the visit matters.”
  • Courteous, compassionate, and respectful ministry drivers
  • Clean, comfortable, and safe transportation van
  • Time to grieve, reflect, and pray — at your own pace
  • No one turned away because of transportation barriers or financial hardship

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Memories In Motion Transportation Ministry exists to provide compassionate transportation and caring companionship that enables individuals and families to honor, remember, and remain connected to their loved ones. We are committed to removing transportation, mobility, and financial barriers so that every person has the opportunity to visit, reflect, and cherish the memories of those they hold dear, because every memory deserves a visit.

Our Vision

To create a community where every individual, regardless of age, physical ability, or financial circumstance, can maintain meaningful connections to loved ones through visits to cemeteries, memorial sites, and places of remembrance. Through compassionate service and companionship, we seek to bring comfort, healing, dignity, and hope to those navigating grief and remembrance.

What We Offer

Services & Programs

Every trip is planned around you — your loved one, your schedule, and your comfort.

Cemetery Visitation Rides

Round-trip, door-to-door transportation to local cemeteries, mausoleums, and memorial gardens across Northeast Ohio.

Veteran Cemetery Visits

Respectful trips to veteran and national cemeteries, including the Ohio Western Reserve National Cemetery.

Holiday & Anniversary Visits

Memorial Day, Veterans Day, birthdays, anniversaries, Mother's & Father's Day — the days that matter most.

Companion Support

A caring presence by your side — a listening ear, a steady arm, and prayer upon request.

Mobility Assistance

Help getting in and out of the van and walking to the gravesite, with patience and care every step of the way.

Flower & Memorial Stops

Stop by a florist or store on the way to pick up flowers, wreaths, flags, or seasonal decorations.

Family & Group Trips

Bring the family together for a shared visit, reunion, or remembrance gathering — we'll handle the driving.

Funeral & Graveside Services

Dependable transportation to funerals, memorial services, and graveside committal services.
