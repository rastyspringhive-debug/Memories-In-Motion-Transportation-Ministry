No One Should Miss a Visit Because of Cost

Visiting a loved one's resting place is an act of deep remembrance, healing, and respect. We believe finances should never stand in the way of honoring someone you love.

If transportation costs are a concern, please speak with us. Through the generosity of our donors, church partners, and community sponsors, assistance is available so that every person who wishes to visit may do so with dignity and care.

About Our Ministry

More Than a Ride — A Ministry of Remembrance

Memories In Motion Transportation Ministry was born from a simple truth: love does not end when a life does.

For many seniors, widows, veterans, and families, visiting a loved one's resting place is a deeply meaningful act, yet age, health, distance, lack of transportation, or financial hardship can keep them from going. We exist to close those gaps with patience, reverence, and genuine care, ensuring that every person has the opportunity to visit, remember, and honor a loved one regardless of their financial circumstances.

“Many people need not only a ride to the cemetery to visit their loved one, but someone who understands why the visit matters.”
  • Courteous, compassionate, and respectful drivers
  • Clean, comfortable, and safe transportation van
  • Time to grieve, reflect, and pray — at your pace

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide compassionate, dependable, and dignified transportation for individuals and families who wish to visit the resting places of their loved ones — honoring every memory, every veteran, and every heart that still longs to remember.

Our Vision

A community where no one is kept from visiting a loved one because of age, disability, distance, financial hardship, or lack of transportation, where every memory is honored with a visit, and every visitor is met with kindness.

What We Offer

Services & Programs

Every trip is planned around you — your loved one, your schedule, and your comfort.

Cemetery Visitation Rides

Round-trip, door-to-door transportation to local cemeteries, mausoleums, and memorial gardens across Northeast Ohio.

Veteran Cemetery Visits

Respectful trips to veteran and national cemeteries, including the Ohio Western Reserve National Cemetery.

Holiday & Anniversary Visits

Memorial Day, Veterans Day, birthdays, anniversaries, Mother's & Father's Day — the days that matter most.

Companion Support

A caring presence by your side — a listening ear, a steady arm, and prayer upon request.

Mobility Assistance

Help getting in and out of the van and walking to the gravesite, with patience and care every step of the way.

Flower & Memorial Stops

Stop by a florist or store on the way to pick up flowers, wreaths, flags, or seasonal decorations.

Family & Group Trips

Bring the family together for a shared visit, reunion, or remembrance gathering — we'll handle the driving.

Funeral & Graveside Services

Dependable transportation to funerals, memorial services, and graveside committal services.

Honoring Those Who Served

A Special Calling to Our Veterans & Their Families

Our nation's veterans gave so much. Their resting places deserve to be visited, and their families deserve a way to get there. We proudly provide transportation to veteran and national cemeteries, so spouses, children, and fellow servicemembers can pay their respects.
