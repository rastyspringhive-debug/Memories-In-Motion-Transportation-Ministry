Ministry Programs

Thoughtful Care for Every Visit

Whether visiting a local community cemetery or traveling to a national veterans memorial, we serve seniors, widows, veterans, and families with patience and respect.

Cemetery Visitation Rides

Round-trip, door-to-door transportation to local cemeteries, mausoleums, and memorial gardens across Solon and Northeast Ohio.

Veteran Cemetery Visits

Respectful trips to veteran and national cemeteries, including the Ohio Western Reserve National Cemetery in Seville, Ohio.

Holiday & Anniversary Visits

Memorial Day, Veterans Day, birthdays, wedding anniversaries, Mother's Day, and Father's Day — the days that matter most.

Companion Support

A caring presence by your side — a listening ear, a steady arm, help locating markers, and quiet prayer upon request.

Mobility Assistance

Help getting in and out of the van and walking safely to the gravesite, with patience and care every step of the way.

Flower & Memorial Stops

Stop by a florist or store on the way to pick up fresh flowers, wreaths, flags, or seasonal decorations, plus assistance placing them.

Family & Group Trips

Bring the family together for a shared visit, memorial anniversary, or remembrance gathering — we will handle the driving.

Funeral & Graveside Services

Dependable transportation for seniors and family members attending funerals, memorial services, and graveside committal services.

Your Comfort & Peace of Mind

What to Expect When You Ride With Us

Every visit is unhurried, personal, and tailored to your needs.

We understand that visiting a loved one's resting place is an emotional and sacred moment. Our drivers never rush your visit—we remain on-site so you can take the time your heart needs.

  • Doorstep pickup and safe return to your home or senior residence
  • Gentle step-in support and walker/folding-aid accommodation
  • Optional graveside escort or quiet personal privacy
  • Financial hardship assistance available through our Sponsorship Fund
