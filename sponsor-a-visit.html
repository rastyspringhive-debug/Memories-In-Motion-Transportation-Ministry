Transportation Sponsorship Program

Helping Every Heart Reach Their Loved One's Resting Place

A gift to Memories In Motion Transportation Ministry helps ensure that no senior, widow, veteran, or family member is prevented from visiting a loved one because of financial circumstances. Sponsorship donations directly support transportation assistance for those in need.

Donations help provide compassionate rides for:

  • Seniors
  • Widows and widowers
  • Veterans
  • Individuals with disabilities
  • Families experiencing financial hardship
