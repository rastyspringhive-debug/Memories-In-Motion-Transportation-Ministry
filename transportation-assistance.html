Memories In Motion Transportation Sponsorship Fund

A Transportation Sponsorship Fund has been established to assist individuals experiencing financial hardship. Contributions from churches, funeral homes, businesses, and community members help ensure that every memory deserves a visit.

Who Assistance Supports

Sponsorship donations directly support transportation assistance for seniors, widows and widowers, veterans, individuals with disabilities, and families experiencing financial hardship.
