A Sacred Commitment

A Special Calling to Our Veterans & Their Families

Founded from personal experience following the loss of a beloved husband and veteran, Memories In Motion Transportation Ministry holds a special sacred calling to serve veterans, military families, and gold-star widows.

We proudly provide round-trip, door-to-door transportation to veteran and national cemeteries across Ohio. For many spouses, widows, aging parents, and fellow servicemembers, driving the distance to a national cemetery can become difficult. Our ministry bridges that distance with reverence, patience, and heartfelt care—ensuring that every veteran's memory is honored with a visit.

Dignified Support

How We Serve Veteran Families

Every trip to honor a fallen servicemember is handled with the respect their service deserves.

Grave Locator & Escort

National cemeteries span hundreds of acres. We assist you in finding your loved one's exact section and marker and walk beside you to the headstone.

Patriotic Remembrance Days

We coordinate special trips for Memorial Day, Veterans Day, Armed Forces Day, and service anniversaries so no hero is forgotten.

Veterans Organization Partnerships

We welcome partnerships with veterans' organizations, VFW and American Legion posts, churches, and donors to sponsor visits for veterans and widows.
